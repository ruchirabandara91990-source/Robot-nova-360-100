# ROBOT NOVA 360 - 100 🤖

A multi-functional smart service robot developed by integrating modern electronic components, sensors, control systems, and automation technologies.

- **Status:** ✅ **COMPLETED**
- **Developer:** H. M. Ruchira Kanishka Bandara

## 🌟 1. Project Overview
**ROBOT NOVA 360 - 100** is a versatile service robot designed to support a range of tasks in environments such as hospitals, homes, and other public or private facilities. Powered primarily by electricity with solar energy as an additional power source, the robot combines advanced electronic control with robust mechanical design.

---

## ⚙️ 2. Mechanical Structure and Mobility
- **Structure:** The upper and lower sections are constructed using metal plates and box-section bars, providing secure mounting space for electronic components and functional modules.
- **Power & Drive:** The lower section houses a 3000 mAh battery, two drive motors, and key electronic sensors.
- **Mobility:** The drive system is engineered to support forward, backward, and sideways movement.

---

## ✨ 3. Key Features and Capabilities

- **🗣️ Voice Assistant:** Google Assistant integration enables users to request information and interact using voice commands.
- **🛡️ Automatic Sanitization:** Equipped with an automatic sanitization system and disinfectant sprayer to sterilize the surrounding environment.
- **🧹 Detachable Cleaning System:** Features a removable cleaning module including an integrated vacuum cleaner.
- **📱 Smartphone Display:** A front-mounted mobile phone provides access to smartphone functions and entertainment applications, including games.
- **💡 Automatic Night Light:** Automatically illuminates surrounding areas in dark conditions.
- **📷 Camera & Communication:** Supports remote observation and communication, including doctor-patient interactions.
- **🎙️ Voice Recording & Remote Control:** Includes voice-recording functionality and a camera designed for remote operation.
- **🚨 Security & Safety Systems:** Features an integrated security system, fire-detection functionality, and a gas-leak warning system.
- **🎵 Sound System:** Built-in audio system for music playback and entertainment.
- **🧭 Advanced Navigation Systems:** 
  * Human-Following Navigation (detects and follows individuals)
  * Remote-Controlled Operation
  * Line-Following Path Navigation
  * Optical Obstacle Detection & Avoidance
- **⚖️ Terrain Stability:** Engineered to maintain balance when operating near slopes, inclines, and staircases.
- **🔌 Utility:** Includes a charging adapter allowing users to charge mobile phones.
